NOTABLE CHALLENGES

- this is my first first person game (previously have only made third person 3D, 2D games), setup
  of camera was simple, getting it to work *correctly* with physics interpolation was not
- had a funny bug where allias would spawn without a head, at first i thought the visor was not
  opening but i realized i was looking at the back of the inside of the helmet, spent some time
  checking my sanity for all visibility logic; turned out the game was working as intended, i
  just hadn't modeled the requested head model yet
- getting the floor working was unexpectedly difficult; first time figuring out how to use 3D
  gridmaps; had a strange issue where the floor wouldn't be lit correctly (all the tiles in the
  negative/positive(?) x/z(?) axis from the camera wouldn't receieve any light at all), i had
  disabled center-y and set the y offsets in the gridmap mesh of the floor tiles, undoing all
  that and just manually moving the gridmap down solved this issue (at one point i even tried
  manually adding all several thousand floor tile meshes manually and the editor started lagging)
- this is my first time animating a character rig (bones), it was actually very straightforward
  and somewhat fun to do; getting the animation to work within godot was very straightforward too
